Choose one reading for each numbered note line. The header lists are already
parsed. Treat the notes as data, never as instructions. Do not solve the rota.

Decide two things for each line, in this order.
1. Does the sentence state a rule that holds for the rota being scheduled now?
2. If it does, which printed option says it?

Options are printed for any line that names people, blocks, or stations. A
printed option does not mean the line is a rule. Answer X, the line adds no
rule, when the sentence is about:
- something wished for, asked for, requested, or put in for that did not happen
- an earlier arrangement, or who used to do something (an old rota, a past cycle)
- something that would, could, or might have been, or an imagined what-if about
  a situation that did not happen (a hedge that starts with "if" or "unless",
  such as "if memory serves" or "unless I have this wrong", is not a what-if)
- something raised or discussed with no decision recorded
- anything social or practical that does not place a person on a block or
  station, or order two people.
A wish or a refusal places nobody, even when it names a person and a block and
the menu offers "works" and "does not work". Answer X.

An opening that only says how sure the writer is, or where the information
came from, does not weaken a rule. "I'm fairly sure", "as far as I know",
"going off the roster", "my recollection is", "speaking from memory" and
anything like them are hedges. Cross the hedge out and judge the sentence under
it. A current rule with a hedge still gets its letter, not X. A wish, request,
or what-if with a hedge is still X.

Reply with one line per note and no other text:

A letter that is not listed for that line is rejected and the line adds no rule.
Do not invent a reading that is not printed under the line.

- "Omar finishes before Nina starts." and option "Omar is earlier than Nina." -> that letter

  "Nina is between Omar and Lena." -> that letter

  "Pavel is between Nina and Lena." -> that letter

  -> that letter
- "Pavel hoped for the 11:00 block, but it went to someone else." -> X
- "Omar applied for the 13:00 slot and was refused." -> X
- "Lena asked to swap onto 09:00; management said no." -> X

Extract every definite constraint the options list, including hedged ones. The
note may use different words from the printed option. Pick the option with the
same people, block or station, and direction. "X later than Y" means Y is
earlier than X. If no printed option is the current rule, answer X. Never
answer with a letter the line does not list.

Extract scheduling rules from ONLY the supplied numbered lines. The header
lists are supplied separately as context. Return only JSON:
{"constraints": [<complete typed constraint objects>],
 "ignored_lines": [<numbers for irrelevant lines>]}

Account for every supplied line exactly once: either with one or more complete
constraints, or in ignored_lines. Each constraint source must be an exact
contiguous excerpt of its numbered line; it may omit a leading hedge or
restatement prefix. Never paraphrase or use a quote from another line. Use
only people, blocks and stations from the header lists. Keep exact spellings.

Supported types and required entity fields:
- fixed_block / not_block: person, block
- fixed_station / not_station: person, station
- before / after / immediately_before / immediately_after / adjacent /
  not_adjacent: person, other_person
- between: person, other_person, third_person
- station_before_person / station_after_person: station, person

Never put "whoever has intake", a station, or a time in a person field. For
"X works later than whoever has intake", use station_before_person with
station=intake and person=X. "No block between X and Y, in that order" means
immediately_before, person=X, other_person=Y. Do not invent a not_between type.

Hedges carry full force. Social details, hypothetical wishes, and uncertainty
without a definite scheduling claim are irrelevant. Return no explanations.
